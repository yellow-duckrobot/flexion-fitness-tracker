# FLEXION — User Guide

## Getting Started

1. Open the app and click **Get Started**
2. Fill in your name, username, email, password and accept the privacy policy
3. Log in — you'll land on your **Dashboard**

## Dashboard

Your command center:

- **Fitness Score** — computed live from your workouts, calories and progress entries
- **Stat tiles** — streak, weekly workouts, today's calories, current weight
- **7-Day Training Volume** — how much you lifted each day
- **Nutrition Today** — macro donut (protein/carbs/fat)
- **Consistency Heatmap** — 12 weeks of activity; darker = more active
- **🔔 Bell** — notifications of your recent activity
- **Search bar** — find workouts, meals and other users
- **Hydration** — tap the glasses to log water (8/8 = confetti!)

## Tracking Workouts

Workouts → **+ New workout** → name it, pick a category, add exercises (sets/reps/weight), tag it, save.
Edit with the ✏️ icon, delete with 🗑️. Filter by category or search by name.

## Logging Meals

Nutrition → **Log meal** → pick meal type + date, add food items (or tap **Quick Add** chips) → Log meal.
Watch your macro donut and calorie bar update. Browse other days with the ◀ ▶ arrows.

## Tracking Progress

Progress → **Log entry** → record weight, measurements (chest/waist/hips/arm), max lift or 5K time.
Charts draw automatically. View history at the bottom.

## Reminders

Settings → **Enable notifications** → toggle workout/meal/goal reminders → set your times.
The app notifies you at those times (keep the tab open).

## Appearance & Units

Settings → **Appearance** (Dark/Light) and **Units** (kg·cm / lb·in) — saved to your account.

## Mini Game 🎮

"Snack or Stack" in the sidebar — guess which food has more calories. 4 modes, high scores saved.

## AI Coach 🤖

Floating button (bottom-right, every page) — ask about losing weight, gaining muscle, protein,
sleep, or how any feature works. Try "what can you do?"

## Export & Privacy

Settings → **Export my data** (CSV files) or use Profile to manage your info.
Full JSON export and account deletion are available via the API (`/api/users/me/export`, DELETE `/api/users/me`).
